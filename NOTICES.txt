EEA vehicle reference data
--------------------------

The car search uses an adapted snapshot of EEA's Monitoring of CO2 emissions
from passenger cars, 2024 – Final. Copyright: Directorate-General for Climate
Action (DG CLIMA), licensed CC BY 4.0. The full licence is retained in
THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES/CC-BY-4.0.txt. The attributed derived reference catalogue is bundled in the Android
app and is separate from its MIT source licence. No EEA or DG CLIMA endorsement
is implied. Records are grouped by technical characteristics; reported fuel
consumption ranges are retained, and missing or implausible fields remain
unavailable. Further provenance and transformations: catalogue/README.md.

https://www.eea.europa.eu/en/datahub/datahubitem-view/fa8b1229-3db6-495d-b18e-9c9b3267c02b
https://sdi.eea.europa.eu/catalogue/srv/api/records/5018ec17-2348-4c92-8761-6f2377bbd1c0/formatters/xml
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

EPA body-class reference
-----------------------

Vehicle body estimates use a reduced 2024 EPA/DOE FuelEconomy.gov size-class
reference. EPA-produced data is in the US public domain under 17 USC 105;
the EPA supplies no accuracy or utility warranty. No agency endorsement is
implied. Only names, identifiers, year and size class are retained. Exact
name matching and app-category mapping are adaptations, not verified European
trim specifications. Fuel, mass and consumption continue to come from EEA.
Renault's manufacturer description supplies the separate Clio V classification.

https://www.fueleconomy.gov/feg/download.shtml
https://edg.epa.gov/EPA_Data_License.html
https://www.press.renault.co.uk/releases/2612

Android 0.9.0 also includes jsoup 1.23.2 (MIT, Jonathan Hedley) to parse
the current official GOV.SI fuel-price table, and Android desugar_jdk_libs_nio
2.1.5 for Java library compatibility (OpenJDK GPLv2 with Classpath Exception
and the accompanying additional licence/assembly information). Their notices
are retained in THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES/.
https://jsoup.org/
https://github.com/google/desugar_jdk_libs

The Android FTS4 catalogue is a derived form of the same attributed EEA/EPA
inputs described above, carrying the same source and body-estimate provenance.
